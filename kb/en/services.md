# Services overview

Acme CRM Onboarding helps B2B teams migrate to or roll out a new CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Close, or Zoho) without losing data, deals, or momentum. We focus on companies with 10 to 200 employees who are either replacing a CRM that isn't working or moving off spreadsheets for the first time.

## What we do

- **CRM selection** — a 1-week guided sprint to choose the right CRM for your size, GTM motion, and tooling.
- **Data migration** — clean, dedupe, and import contacts, companies, deals, activities, and historical notes from your old system.
- **Pipeline and stage design** — translate how your team actually sells into deal stages, automations, and required fields.
- **Team enablement** — playbooks and live training so your reps actually use the CRM after we leave.
- **30-day post-launch support** — included with every engagement; weekly office hours and async help in Slack or email.

## What we don't do

- Build custom apps on top of the CRM (we'll recommend a partner if you need this).
- Full marketing automation buildouts (we cover lead capture into the CRM, but not nurture sequences).
- Ongoing managed services beyond the 30-day support window.

## Who it's for

A typical client has 10–200 employees, 2–20 sales reps, and is moving from spreadsheets, a CRM that the team has abandoned, or a CRM the founders chose at company size 3 and have outgrown. We work mostly with SaaS, B2B services, and light manufacturing companies.
