# Pricing

We offer three packages. All prices are flat fees in USD, billed 50% on signing and 50% on go-live. No hourly billing surprises.

## Packages at a glance

| Package | Price | Best for | Typical timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter (least expensive) | $4,500 | Under 5 sales reps | 2–3 weeks |
| Growth | $9,500 | 5–15 reps | 4–6 weeks |
| Scale (most expensive, most complete) | $18,000 | 15–50 reps or complex GTM | 8–10 weeks |

## Starter — $4,500

Price: $4,500 flat fee. Best for teams with under 5 sales reps moving off spreadsheets or a free CRM tier.

- CRM selection workshop (HubSpot or Pipedrive recommended at this size).
- Up to 5,000 contacts and 500 deals migrated.
- 2 sales pipelines with up to 6 stages each.
- 1 live training session (90 minutes) recorded for your team.
- 30 days of post-launch support.
- Typical timeline: 2–3 weeks.

## Growth — $9,500

Price: $9,500 flat fee. Best for teams with 5–15 reps replacing an existing CRM.

- Everything in Starter.
- Up to 25,000 contacts and 5,000 deals migrated, including historical activities.
- Custom property and lifecycle stage design.
- Up to 3 sales pipelines + 1 customer success pipeline.
- 2 live training sessions + manager playbook.
- Integration with one tool you already use (Slack, Linear, accounting, support).
- Typical timeline: 4–6 weeks.

## Scale — $18,000

Price: $18,000 flat fee, our most expensive and most complete package. Best for teams with 15–50 reps or complex GTM motions (PLG + sales-assist, multi-region, channel partners).

- Everything in Growth.
- Up to 100,000 contacts and unlimited deals.
- Multi-team pipelines with role-based views.
- Up to 5 integrations including bi-directional sync.
- 4 live training sessions across roles (AE, AM, CS, ops).
- Quarterly 60-min check-in for 12 months after launch.
- Typical timeline: 8–10 weeks.

## Add-ons

- **Extra integration**: $1,200 per tool beyond the package limit.
- **Extended support**: $1,800/month for managed support beyond the included 30 days.
- **Custom reporting build**: $2,500 flat fee.

## What's not included

- CRM license fees (you pay the CRM vendor directly).
- Email deliverability or DNS setup.
- Marketing automation tools.

If your needs don't fit neatly into a package, we'll scope a fixed price after a 30-minute discovery call.
