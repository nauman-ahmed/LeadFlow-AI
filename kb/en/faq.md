# Frequently asked questions

## Which CRMs do you support?

HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Close, and Zoho. We've done dozens of HubSpot and Pipedrive engagements; Salesforce mostly for teams of 30+. If you're considering Monday Sales CRM or Attio, we can advise but typically refer you to a specialist.

## We're already on a CRM but it's a mess. Can you fix it?

Yes — that's roughly 60% of our work. We call this a **CRM reset**: audit, clean data, redesign pipelines, retrain the team. Pricing typically falls into the Growth package.

## How long until we're live?

Starter: 2–3 weeks. Growth: 4–6 weeks. Scale: 8–10 weeks. We can move faster for urgent cases but data migration quality usually limits the lower bound.

## Do you sign NDAs?

Yes, we sign a mutual NDA before the discovery call if needed.

## Will you migrate notes and call recordings?

Notes and activities yes, on every package. Call recordings: technically possible but rarely worth the storage cost — we usually recommend leaving them in your old call system and adding a link.

## Can you work with our data residency requirements?

We work with EU-only and US-only data residency. For HIPAA, SOC 2 type II, or other regulated environments, we partner with a specialist firm — let us know and we'll introduce you.

## What if we change CRMs after you've launched us?

We charge half the original engagement fee for a migration to a different CRM within 24 months. Outside that window, you'd start a new engagement.

## Do you take equity or revenue share?

No. Flat fees only. We're consultants, not investors.

## What's your refund policy?

If we haven't started discovery interviews yet, full refund. After interviews and before migration, 50% refund. After migration, no refund but we'll always fix issues we caused.

## Who would we actually work with?

Our two founders, Maya and Jules, lead every engagement. They bring in a junior associate for data migration on Growth and Scale packages.
