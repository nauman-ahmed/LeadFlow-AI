# Engagement process

Every engagement runs in four phases. Total duration is 2–10 weeks depending on the package.

## Week 1 — Discovery and selection

We run a 60-minute kickoff with your founders or sales leadership, then 30-minute interviews with 2–4 reps. We map your current sales motion, must-haves, nice-to-haves, and dealbreakers. By end of week 1 you have a written CRM recommendation with rationale and a migration plan.

## Week 2–4 — Build

We provision the CRM, design pipelines and properties, set up users and permissions, configure required fields and automation, and connect any in-scope integrations. You get progress updates every 48 hours in a shared Slack channel or weekly check-ins by email if you prefer async.

## Week 3–5 — Data migration

We export your existing data, clean it (deduplication, normalization, owner reassignment), and stage it in a sandbox import. You review a sample import, sign off, then we run the full production import. Migration always happens on a Friday evening so Monday opens with the new CRM live.

## Week 4–6 — Training and go-live

Live training sessions (recorded) are delivered the week before go-live. Each role gets a short, role-specific playbook. Go-live is the Monday after migration. We're in your Slack live for the first 5 business days for any hiccups.

## 30 days after go-live

Weekly office hours for 4 weeks. Async help available throughout. If something's broken, we fix it; if something needs adjusting, we adjust it. After 30 days we hand off cleanly with documentation and an admin guide.

We don't take on a new client during week 4 of an existing engagement, so we are sometimes 2–3 weeks out before we can start. We'll always tell you up front when we can begin.
